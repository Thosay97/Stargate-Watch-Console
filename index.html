Full Chronological Watch Order

This checklist covers the whole franchise from the very beginning: the original 1994 film, every episode of Stargate SG-1 seasons 1 through 10, Stargate Atlantis seasons 1 through 5, the two follow-up movies (The Ark of Truth and Continuum), Stargate Universe, and the optional Stargate Origins web series. Where SG-1 and Atlantis overlap, episodes are interleaved in air-date order so crossovers and shared storylines land correctly. Episode descriptions are short paraphrases based on public episode guides. Check off each entry as you go, filter by series, switch between light and dark glass themes, and use the sync code below to pick up where you left off on another device.
