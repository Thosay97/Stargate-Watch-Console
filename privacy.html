Stargate Watch Console is a fan-made episode tracker. This page explains what data the site touches and why.

What's stored locally

Your watch progress, spoiler preference, and sound preference are saved in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device unless you use the sync feature below.

Cross-device sync

If you use the sync code feature, a randomly generated code (not tied to your name, email, or any account) and your checklist progress are stored on a Redis database so you can load the same progress on another device. Anyone who has your sync code can view or overwrite that progress, so treat it like a lightweight password and don't share it publicly.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad personalization settings at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
