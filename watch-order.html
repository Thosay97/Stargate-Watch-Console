Frequently asked questions

What is the chronological watch order for Stargate?

Start with the 1994 film, then watch Stargate SG-1 from season 1. From mid 2004 SG-1 and Stargate Atlantis air side by side, so this tracker interleaves them by original air date. Crossover episodes land next to each other, then the SG-1 movies (The Ark of Truth and Continuum), Stargate Universe and the optional Origins web series follow.

Do I have to watch SG-1 before Stargate Atlantis?

Not strictly. Atlantis is written so new viewers can follow it. The two shows share characters, mythology and a few direct crossovers, so watching them in air date order is the safest way to avoid spoiling either series.

How many Stargate episodes and movies are there?

This checklist has 367 entries: 3 movies (the 1994 original, The Ark of Truth and Continuum), 214 SG-1 episodes, 100 Stargate Atlantis episodes, 40 Stargate Universe episodes and 10 Stargate Origins episodes.

Is Stargate Universe or Stargate Origins required?

No. Universe is a separate crew on a separate ship and mostly stands alone. Origins is an optional prequel web series set in the 1930s. Both are included at the end of the list so you can skip them.

How do I keep my place and avoid spoilers?

Tap any row to check it off. Use Up Next to see what to watch next, and use Hide spoilers to blur episode summaries until you reveal them. If you are already partway through, tap Mark all watched up to here on an episode to catch up in one go.
